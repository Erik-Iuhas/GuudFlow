# Decide, block or fail

- Decide yourself when the brief, the spec files or the design answer the question, or when a
  sensible, reversible choice exists. Note the assumption in your summary.
- A question about the spec or the design: finish `blocked` with `blocked_on` the tech lead and
  exactly what you need. The overseer answers it.
- Block only on a teammate who must change something you cannot. Never block on an owner check,
  a missing screenshot or reference, or a refused command: run the allowed part alone instead.
- Finish `failed` with what is done, what is left and why, when you cannot finish.
- Nobody answers questions mid-turn and you cannot talk to other agents. Use `notes_for` to
  leave a teammate a note.
